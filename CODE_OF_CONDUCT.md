# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, welcoming, and productive place for
everyone who wants to contribute, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. The full text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas
rather than people, and help keep this a space where others can collaborate on
the work.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this standard, please report
it by opening a confidential issue or by contacting the maintainer through their
GitHub profile ([@swapnildahiphale](https://github.com/swapnildahiphale)). All
reports will be reviewed and handled in good faith, and the reporter's privacy
will be respected.
